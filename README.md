# What Are the Quickest Natural Ways to Lose Weight?

The fastest way to naturally lose weight that you can actually keep is a steady calorie gap of roughly 500 to 750 calories a day, built from filling whole foods, extra protein and fiber, regular movement and enough sleep.

That usually works out to about 0.5 kg to 1 kg (1 to 2 pounds) a week, the pace the NHS and CDC both describe as safe.

Anything much faster tends to cost you muscle, energy and, often, the weight itself when it comes back.

This guide covers how to lose weight fast without wrecking your health, how to curb cravings, what to do about belly fat, whether you can lose weight without exercise, and when to talk to a doctor.

It is general information, not medical advice.

## The NHS Safe Rate: 0.5 kg to 1 kg a Week

Searches such as NHS safe weight loss 0.5kg to 1kg per week, NHS healthy weight loss 0.5kg to 1kg per week and NHS safe rate of weight loss 0.5 to 1 kg per week point to one source.

The NHS 12-week[ weight loss guide](https://assets.nhs.uk/tools/download-panels/data/weight-loss/pdf/all-weeks.pdf) says its steps help you work toward losing weight "at a safe and sustainable rate of 0.5kg to 1kg (1lb to 2lbs) a week."

The PDF shows no date, so confirm it is current.

Its weekly steps include tracking food and activity, eating more fiber, controlling portions, planning snacks, watching drinks, building up to 150 minutes of moderate activity a week and adding muscle-strengthening twice a week.

The guide uses daily limits of about 1,900 kcal for men and 1,400 kcal for women; needs vary, so ask a doctor or dietitian first.

## What the CDC Says: 1 to 2 Pounds a Week

The CDC's page on[ steps for losing weight](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/losing-weight/index.html), last updated January 17, 2025, says to lose weight gradually, about 1 to 2 pounds per week, and that people who lose at that pace are more likely to keep it off.

That is the source behind CDC healthy weight loss 1 to 2 pounds per week and CDC safe weight loss 1 to 2 pounds per week.

It warns that unrealistic goals, such as losing 20 pounds in 2 weeks, can be discouraging, and says even 5% weight loss can improve blood pressure, cholesterol and blood sugar.

## Why Faster Is Not Always Better

If you wonder how to lose weight fast naturally and permanently, the key word is permanently.

A Healthline article on[ losing weight too fast](https://healthline.com/nutrition/losing-weight-too-fast), updated September 10, 2025, written by Ryan Raman, MS, RD and reviewed by Jillian Foglesong Stabile, MD, lists these risks:

* Loss of muscle and bone density
* Gallstones
* Nutrient deficiencies, including protein, iron, vitamin B12 and calcium
* A slower metabolism
* Hormonal changes
* Fatigue and mood changes
* Regaining the weight, since very low-calorie diets are hard to sustain

It recommends about 1 to 2 pounds (0.45 to 0.9 kg) a week, which matches a daily deficit of roughly 500 to 750 calories.

Very low-calorie plans should only be done under medical supervision.

## Can You Lose Weight Without Exercise?

Can you lose weight without exercise? Yes.

Diet alone can create a calorie gap, so how to lose weight without exercising, how to lose weight fast without exercise and how to lose weight without working out are all possible on paper.

The trade-off is that you may lose more muscle and find it harder to keep the weight off.

The NHS says moving more helps you keep weight off, and you can start with 10 minutes at a time.

If workouts feel impossible, walk more, take stairs, stand while on calls and do chores briskly.

## How to Burn Fat and Belly Fat

People ask how to burn fat, how to burn fat fast, how to lose fat fast and how to lose body fat fast, often hoping for a belly fat burner.

No food, drink or exercise burns fat from one spot.

An InBody USA blog post (updated August 14, 2025), from a company that sells body composition devices, calls spot reduction a myth and cites a University of Connecticut study in which 12 weeks of training one arm caused no fat loss in either arm on MRI.

It also cites a 2011 study in which six weeks of abdominal training did not reduce abdominal subcutaneous fat.

So the best way to lose belly fat, and how to get rid of belly fat, is overall fat loss with strength training, walking and a calorie gap.

Treat the vendor source with care, but the advice matches mainstream guidance.

## How to Lose Body Fat Fast: Female Readers

How to lose body fat fast is a common search.

There is no special fast method for women.

The same pace of 0.5 to 1 kg a week applies.

Hormone changes around periods, pregnancy and menopause can affect weight and appetite, so see a doctor if your weight shifts quickly or your cycle changes.

That is general knowledge, not from a source I checked here.

Be careful with searches such as how to get skinny fast.

If you feel pressure to lose weight very quickly, skip meals or fear eating, please speak to a doctor or counselor.

Fast, extreme loss can harm your body and your relationship with food.

## Weight Loss at Home and Home Remedies

Weight loss at home is practical: cook more, keep treats out of sight, use smaller plates, drink water, walk after meals and do short bodyweight workouts.

It also answers the search for how to lose weight fastly in home. You can't rush biology, but you can set up your kitchen to help.

The NHS page lists 10-minute home workout videos for beginners.

Home remedies for weight loss such as lemon water, jeera water or ginger tea are popular, but I found no reliable source showing they burn fat.

They are fine as low-calorie drinks, not as a method.

## Sleep, Stress and Daily Habits

The NHS Better Health page says poor sleep can lead to higher-calorie food choices and less energy to exercise or cook.

## A Simple One-Week Starter Plan

Use this plan to start losing weight without overhauling your life:

1. Day 1: write down everything you eat and drink.
2. Day 2: swap sugary drinks for water or unsweetened tea.
3. Day 3: add a vegetable and a protein to every meal.
4. Day 4: take a 15-minute walk, the CDC's starter example.
5. Day 5: plan next week's meals and snacks.
6. Day 6: do 10 minutes of strength work at home.
7. Day 7: review what worked and set two or three goals.

## When to See a Doctor

See a doctor before a big diet change if you have diabetes, heart, kidney or thyroid disease, take regular medicines, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of an eating disorder.

Get help if weight loss is unplanned, you feel faint, or you can't stop thinking about food and weight.

## Frequently Asked Questions

A steady calorie gap of roughly 500 to 750 a day, which gives about 0.5 to 1 kg (1 to 2 pounds) a week, with protein, fiber, movement and sleep.
